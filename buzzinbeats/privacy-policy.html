Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Buzzin Beats ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Buzzin Beats mobile application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your email address, display name, and encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Preferences: Your music genre preferences, playlist selections, and app settings.
  • Usage Data: Tracks played, playlists created, session duration, and interaction patterns.
  • Payment Information: We process payments through third-party providers. We do not store your card or banking details.
  • Technical Data: App version and basic device information for troubleshooting.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • Personalise your music experience based on your preferences.
  • Process subscription payments securely through third-party providers.
  • Send you in-app announcements and recommendations.
  • Improve the App through aggregate usage analytics.
  • Respond to your support requests.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the App, and when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days. Payment records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data. You can delete your account directly from within the App. To exercise any rights, contact us at support@buzzinbeats.com.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
