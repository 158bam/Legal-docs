Account Deletion

Buzzin Beats — Music & Entertainment

This page explains how to delete your Buzzin Beats account and what happens to your data after deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the Buzzin Beats app:

1 Open the Buzzin Beats app on your device.

2 Go to Profile tab.

3 Tap on Settings (gear icon).

4 Select "Delete Account" at the bottom.

5 Confirm deletion by following the on-screen prompts.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be irretrievably lost after 30 days.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Immediate effect: Your account is deactivated immediately.
  • 30-day retention period: Your data is preserved for 30 days in case you change your mind.
  • Permanent deletion: After 30 days, all personal data is permanently deleted.
  • Payment records: Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax purposes.
