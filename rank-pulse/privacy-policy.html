Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Rank Pulse ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Rank Pulse mobile application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully. By using the App you agree to the terms described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Your email address, display name, and encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Profile & Preferences: Your chosen exam (e.g. CTET, REET, KVS), paper type, language preference, and preparation level.
  • Study & Performance Data: Questions attempted, correct/incorrect answers, session duration, topic accuracy, mock test scores, streaks, and achievement badges.
  • Payment Information: Razorpay order ID and payment ID when you purchase a subscription. We do NOT store your card, UPI, or bank details — these are handled exclusively by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant).
  • Usage Data: App interactions, notification read/unread status, bookmarked questions, and leaderboard ranking.
  • Technical Data: App version. We do not collect device IDs or precise location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • Personalise your study experience based on your exam and preferences.
  • Process and verify subscription payments through Razorpay.
  • Display your progress, analytics, and leaderboard ranking.
  • Send you in-app announcements, reminders, and achievement notifications.
  • Improve the App through aggregate usage analytics.
  • Respond to your support requests.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Razorpay: Payment details (order ID, amount) are shared with Razorpay to process transactions. Razorpay's privacy policy governs their use of this data.
  • Supabase: Our backend database and authentication provider (Supabase) stores your data on servers located within their infrastructure. Supabase's data processing agreement applies.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it. Payment records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate data via your profile settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data from within the App (Profile → Delete Account). Auth records are permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Portability: Request an export of your personal data by emailing us.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@rankpulse.com.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own data. Payment details are handled exclusively by Razorpay and never pass through our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and above (teacher candidates preparing for exams). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notification. Continued use of the App after the effective date of the revised policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
