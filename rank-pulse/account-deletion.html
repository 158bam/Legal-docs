Account Deletion

Rank Pulse — Teacher Exam Preparation

This page explains how to delete your Rank Pulse account and what happens to your data after deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the Rank Pulse mobile app:

1 Open the Rank Pulse app on your device.

2 Go to Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.

3 Tap on Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner.

4 Select "Delete Account" at the bottom of the settings page.

5 Confirm deletion by following the on-screen prompts.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be irretrievably lost after 30 days.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Immediate effect: Your account is deactivated immediately. You will be logged out and unable to access the app.
  • 30-day retention period: Your data is preserved for 30 days in case you change your mind and contact support to restore your account.
  • Permanent deletion: After 30 days, all your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems.
  • Payment records: Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, as required by law.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account credentials and profile information
  • Study history, performance data, and analytics
  • Mock test scores, streaks, and achievement badges
  • Bookmarked questions and preferences
  • Subscription information and payment identifiers (order IDs)

Data That Will Be Retained (Anonymised)

  • Aggregate usage statistics (not linked to you personally)
  • Payment transaction logs (for legal/tax compliance, up to 7 years)

GDPR Right to Erasure

If you are a resident of the European Union, you have the right to request immediate permanent deletion of your data under GDPR Article 17 ("Right to Erasure"). To exercise this right, contact us at rankpulse159@gmail.com after initiating the in-app deletion process.
